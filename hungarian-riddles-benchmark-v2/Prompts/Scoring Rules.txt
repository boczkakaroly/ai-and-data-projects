Evaluation Rubrics — Scoring Grid
1. Accuracy (0 / / 3 / 5)
Factual accuracy of the Final_Answer
0 = No answer / irrelevant /hallucination
3 = Partially correct / incomplete / imprecise
5 = Fully correct and precise

2. Justification (0–5)
Justification of the Reasoning
0 = Hallucination, invented reasoning
1 = No justification
2 = On-topic but far from correct
3 = Partially correct but drifting / vague
4 = Nearly correct, but verbose or slightly unclear
5 = Concise, logical, and fully adequate

3. Grammar & Style (0–5, broken down as sub-criteria)
Grammar & Style of the Reasoning
Grammar (0–3)
0 = unreadable / not Hungarian
1 = serious grammar issues
2 = understandable, minor errors
3 = correct, fluent grammar
+
Format & Length (0–1)
Style & Tone (0–1)
SUM: 0–5
Auto-0 rule: If Justification=0 or 1 (missing or hallucination) then there is no Grammar & Style score
